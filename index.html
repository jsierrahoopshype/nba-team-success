    • Wins minus lossesHis teams' regular-season wins minus losses in the games he played, every NBA player since 1946-47. It rewards sustained winning and availability, so it favors long careers: switch to win % or filter by era.
    • Winning seasonsSeasons on a team that finished above .500 (the team he played the most games for that season), and their share of his seasons.
    • Playoff tripsSeasons in which he played a playoff game or finished the season with a playoff team, and their share of his seasons. Play-in losses are not trips.
    • Deepest playoff runEvery player's best playoff finish, from a title to missing the playoffs. Equal outcomes share a rank; within a rank, players are listed by how many times they got that far.
    • TitlesChampionships won by the team he played for, counted when he played a playoff game for the champion or finished the season with it.
    • Average final NBA finishThe average final NBA finish (1 = champion) of his teams, one team per season, minimum 5 seasons. The league-size-adjusted view puts every season on one scale, 0 = champion, 1 = last.
    • With vs without: career win-rate gapHis teams' win % in the games he played minus in the games he missed (team-season window), minimum 200 games played and 50 missed, both samples shown.
    • Final standings, every seasonAll teams ranked by final NBA finish, 1946-47 to 2025-26.
