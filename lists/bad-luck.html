    Bad Luck Index: great players on bad teams

    Accolades against team success, plus the ring chasers and the carriers.

    The formula

    Accolade score = the same weights Teammates Score uses, added up over his awards: Finals MVP 10, Most Valuable Player 10, All-NBA First Team 4, Defensive Player of the Year 4, All-NBA Second Team 3, All-NBA Third Team 2, All-Defensive First Team 1, All-Star 1, All-Defensive Second Team 0.5, All-Star MVP 0.5, Rookie of the Year 0.5, Sixth Man of the Year 0.5, All-Rookie First Team 0.25, Most Improved Player 0.25, All-Rookie Second Team 0.125. An MVP already covers that season's All-NBA nod, and a DPOY that season's All-Defensive nod.

    Bad Luck Index = Accolade score × (.500 − his teams' average win %) × 10. His teams' average is weighted by the games he played for each. Only players whose teams averaged under .500, minimum 5 seasons. With an era filter on, both numbers count only seasons in that era.
